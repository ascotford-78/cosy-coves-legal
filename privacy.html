Privacy Policy

Cosy Coves Fishing Co. · Last updated 3 October 2026

Home Privacy Support

Cosy Coves keeps everything on your device. There are no accounts, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking, and we never collect or sell your data.

Overview

Cosy Coves Fishing Co. ("Cosy Coves", "the game") is a fishing and harbour management game made by Scotford Studios. This policy explains what information the game uses and where it goes. We wrote the game for families, so we've kept it simple: we don't collect personal information at all.

Data stored on your device only

Your game progress is saved on your device. This includes player profiles (the name and picture you choose for each player), your boats, money, journal, Catch Collection and settings such as sound and graphics quality. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Player names can be anything you like. They are only used to tell saves apart on the "Who's playing?" screen. If you delete a player in the game, or delete the app, that save is permanently removed.

Game updates

The app checks for small content updates (new fixes and features) by downloading a file from GitHub, which hosts our update files. Like any website, GitHub receives your device's IP address when the file is downloaded, as part of making the connection. We don't send any information about you or your game with this request, and we don't receive or store your IP address. You can read GitHub's privacy statement at docs.github.com.

In-app purchases

On iPhone and iPad, the optional World Pass is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment entirely; we never see your name, Apple ID, email address or payment details. The game only asks the App Store whether the World Pass has been bought on your Apple ID so it can unlock the extra ports. Apple's privacy policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

What we don't collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information
  • We do not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising or tracking of any kind
  • We do not use your device's camera, microphone, photos or location
  • We do not sell or share any data with third parties

Children's privacy

Cosy Coves is suitable for children. Because the game does not collect any personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children. There are no adverts, chat or links to social media in the game. Purchases on iPhone and iPad go through Apple, so parents can use Ask to Buy and Screen Time to control them.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can remove all game data at any time by deleting players in the game or deleting the app.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally, for example if the game gains new features. Any changes will be posted at this address with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at ascotford@gmail.com.
