Frequently asked

Tap your rowing boat, pick a fishing spot and send it out. When it comes home, open the Market to sell your catch to the dealers and restaurants. Save up for a better engine, more boats and crew.

The Journal has a "How to play" tab if you get stuck, and its entries tell you what to aim for next.

While one of your boats is fishing, tap it and press Hop aboard & fish! Cast your line, wait for a bite, then reel in. Hand-caught fish sell for more, and everything you catch goes into your Catch Collection in the Journal, including the junk.
Complete the entries in your Journal. When enough are done, the next port opens on the World Map. Each port has its own fish, boats, buyers and weather.

On iPhone and iPad, the first two ports (Coral Cay and Porto Azzurro) are free. The World Pass is a one-off purchase that lets you sail to the other three: Frostcove, Dutch Harbor and Toyosu. It is not a subscription.

You still unlock each port by completing your Journal; the World Pass just lets you sail there. It covers every player profile on the device.

Open the World Pass screen (from the Menu or the World Map) and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you used to buy it, and that you're connected to the internet.

This also works on a new phone, after reinstalling, and for family members if Family Sharing is turned on.

Your saves are stored on your device only. If you delete the app, your progress is lost (though a World Pass can always be restored). Back up your device before uninstalling if you want to keep your saves.
Open Menu and set Graphics to Fast. Closing other apps can help too.
The game checks for small updates automatically and will offer to restart when one is ready. You can also check from Menu → Check for updates. Bigger updates come through the App Store as usual.
